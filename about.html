About Us

Meet Weston, your local technician

My name is Weston Matthews and I live with my family in Mineola, TX.

I've been fixing and building computers since High School, nearly 20 years ago. I know the ins and outs of PC hardware and software, as well as vintage video game consoles. It started with my own custom PC builds, then fixing my old game consoles, then doing favors for friends and family. Now, I'd like to serve the community and provide a good, honest service that many seem to be in dire need of.
